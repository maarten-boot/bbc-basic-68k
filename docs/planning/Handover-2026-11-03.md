## Recent changes (BBCZ80 log 108-119)

* **File I/O on the SD card:** a FAT sector cache (`bare/fat.c`) and
  sector-aligned buffers (`osl.c`). On the board, 200 random
  PTR#+BPUT# went from 34.6 s to 16.8 s, and FILETEST now finishes.
* **The firmware's 100 Hz tick and DUART set-up are kept** (`bare.c`).
  The garbled console at start-up was BASIC's ACR write.
* **`LOCAL` with no variables does nothing**, as in Acorn's versions
  (`exec2.s`, `test/diff/d15localnone`).
* **`RESTORE DATA`/`ERROR` with nothing to restore:** a difference, not
  a bug (Russell). This port keeps the manual's error, as the user chose.
* **Piped scripts with LF or CR LF line ends now work** (`hostmain.cpp`),
  and `hostcheck` tests them.
